Hi everyone! This is Lucy, and Lucy is my home. I would do anything for her! On a good day, you can find me down here…

In the circulatory system!! I am a very special cell, quite powerful, and I make up an important part of Lucy’s blood because I am a…

A white blood cell (Campbell, 2008)! I watch out for intruders called pathogens. These can be bacteria or viruses. I fight them whenever Lucy gets sick. Here in the bloodstream, you can also see my brothers and sisters, the red blood cells. They carry oxygen to all parts of Lucy’s body! It seems like Lucy is very active at the moment.
